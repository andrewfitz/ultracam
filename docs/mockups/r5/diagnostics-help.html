Ultracam Help
Getting Started
1.Install the Ultracam camera from the sidebar or Settings › Virtual Cameras, and approve it in System Settings.
2.Choose each camera's input device in its inspector (⌥⌘I), then Start Camera.
3.In your meeting app, choose “Ultracam · Camera 1”, or “Ultracam · Program” once you have two cameras.
4.Ultracam's cameras carry video only. Select your microphone in your meeting app.
5.To back up the meeting, choose a recording folder and press Record. Recordings are the camera original, without the LUT or effects.
Good to Know
Clicking a picture selects that camera; it never cuts. Cut with the toolbar, a strip's Cut button or ⌃1–⌃4.
Stopping a camera that is recording or on air asks first.
Record › Pause All pauses every take. When every take is paused, it becomes Resume All.
Changing Output size, Color format, Frame-rate cap or Effects renegotiates the camera format, so the meeting's video updates briefly. The LUT doesn't.
If the on-air camera loses its picture with Auto on, Ultracam cuts to the next live camera, even on Hold. With Auto off it cuts nothing and offers Cut.
Format is in the sidebar and the Record menu. Timecode and folders are in Settings › Recording.
Input connector and camera body pairing are in Settings › Hardware. Channels Shown is in Settings › Audio.
Recording
Program is not recorded. A session leaves each camera's original and a list of the cuts.
A signal loss over 10 s ends the take by design. Everything up to that point is saved.
Takes are always written as fragmented QuickTime movies. The first fragment is written after 1 s, then every 10 s. Hidden .partial files and journals sit beside each take until it finishes.
An interrupted take appears in Attention. Recovery copies complete fragments into a separate _Recovered.mov without re-encoding and keeps the original.
Monitor Tools
Each camera has its own guides, zebra, false color, focus peaking and scopes. Hover a picture for the full tool bar, or use View › Monitor Tools. Tools that are on stay visible, except in Clean View.
New cameras start with monitor tools off. Zebra starts at 95 IRE, and the picture shows the camera's output with its LUT.
Right-click a picture to copy its tools to another camera.
Monitor tools never change camera output or recordings.
Six-band false color works with any source. Sony S-Log3 and ARRI LogC modes require the matching camera log, set in the inspector. Declare what the camera actually sends: declarations don't calibrate the capture device or change camera output.
A Monitor LUT is in the Picture popover. Scope refresh is in the Scopes popover, 15 Hz by default.
Every monitor shows every camera frame with its tools, on the GPU. Only a scope may skip an update while the GPU is still drawing earlier scopes. Hidden or covered monitors do no GPU work.
