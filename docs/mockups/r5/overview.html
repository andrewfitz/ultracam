Typed pieces replace dot-joined text. A value is a strong number with a small dim unit, so 1 h 52 m reads from two feet.
Red needs frames. ON AIR and ISO only show while frames reach a viewer. Lose the on-air picture and the ring and pill turn orange.
Two problem levels. On air or rolling damage is solid amber. Everything else is a quiet wash. Each place says it once, with one action.
Strips never change height. A problem takes the folder row's place, so Cut never moves. LUT stays one click on every strip, expanded or collapsed.
Monitor tools collapse at rest. Enabled tools stay visible as white glyphs, and hovering the picture brings back the full bar in every state.
Switching lives in the toolbar. Auto and Hold fill white when on, which survives a window that isn't key. The sidebar is for setup and can stay closed during a show.
