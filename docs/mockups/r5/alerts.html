Ultracam: dialogs, alerts and notifications

Static mockup with made-up data. Native alerts with the app icon and named verbs. No red: the verb is in label color, and Cancel is the default on anything that ends or rewrites something. Alert sizes were measured on macOS 27.0.1, so macOS 26 is unconfirmed.

Reset All Settings. Settings › Advanced
Reset All Settings?
Cameras, effects, folders, monitor tools and layout return to their defaults. Recordings are not touched. Ultracam quits when it's done.
CancelReset and Quit
Forget Saved LUTs. Settings › Advanced
Forget saved LUTs?
Each camera stops applying its saved LUT and its monitor LUT until you choose a .cube file again.
CancelForget Saved LUTs
Sync cameras. Inspector only, and names the source camera
Sync cameras from Camera 1?
Camera 2 takes Camera 1's effects, LUT, monitor tools, source declarations and preview setting, and meeting apps may see its format change. Inputs and folders stay separate.
CancelSync Cameras
